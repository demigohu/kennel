---
title: Graduation to Rhea
description: The buy that sells out the curve opens a real pool and locks its liquidity, in one transaction.
---

When the curve's 800M tokens are sold (10 NEAR raised), the coin **graduates**: the contract builds a Rhea pool from the raised NEAR and the 200M tokens it kept back, then unleashes the token.

## In one transaction

The buy that sells out the curve runs every step itself. Nobody has to call anything, and there is no window between "curve done" and "pool live".

## The liquidity is locked

The LP shares belong to `kennel.testnet`, and the contract has **no method** that moves, removes or transfers them. Nobody can pull the liquidity: not the pup, not the handler, not us.

## If a step fails

Each step has a callback. If one fails (for example a congested DEX), or the last buyer attached too little gas to run the steps, the coin stays `Graduating` and remembers which steps are done. The indexer's keeper resumes it with `graduate(symbol)` after a minute, and anyone can do the same (`kennel graduate <symbol>`, about 300 TGas). If the token's `unlock` ever fails, `unlock_token(symbol)` retries it. No funds get stuck in between.
