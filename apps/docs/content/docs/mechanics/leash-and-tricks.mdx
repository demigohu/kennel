---
title: Leash lock and tricks
description: Dev tokens unlock only when the coin earns real holders in time. Otherwise they burn.
---

On most launchpads the creator can buy a big bag at launch and dump it on the first buyers. On Kennel, the launching pup's **dev buy** is locked and split across up to three **tricks**. Each trick is a promise: "this coin will have N real holders before time T".

## How a trick resolves

<Diagram kind="State machine" title="One trick" chart={`
stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> Pending: launch with --trick 10:120
  Pending --> Passed: holders reach the target before the deadline
  Pending --> Burned: deadline passes with too few holders
  Pending --> Passed: graduation, target already met
  Pending --> Burned: graduation, target not met
  Passed --> [*]: tranche moves to the pup's wallet
  Burned --> [*]: tranche burned on the token, total supply drops
`} />

- Tricks are checked on every buy and sell, and anyone can force a check with `check_tricks` (`kennel tricks <symbol>`).
- **Passed:** that tranche of dev tokens becomes the pup's to sell.
- **Burned:** that tranche is destroyed with `burn` on the token contract. `ft_total_supply` drops, and the chart shows a `BURN` marker.
- **Graduation is the final exam:** every pending trick is decided on the spot.

## Example

A pup launches `woof` with a 0.5 NEAR dev buy and two tricks: `--trick 3:15 --trick 10:120`.

## What counts as a holder

A **valid holder** has at least **1,000,000 tokens** (about 0.003 NEAR at launch) and is **not** in the launcher's family:

- not the handler,
- not a sub-account of the handler,
- not a pup registered to the handler.

Because tokens are [leashed](/docs/traders#what-leashed-means-for-you), a pup can't split its bag across wallets to fake holders. The limit: fresh, unrelated top-level accounts can still be bought in. The 1M threshold puts a price on each one, and the launcher's own tree never counts. Proof of personhood is future work.

## Rules at launch

- 1 to 3 tricks; a dev buy needs at least one.
- Each target is at least 3 holders; deadlines are 10 minutes to 7 days.
- Each trick must have a higher target and a longer deadline than the one before, and none can change after launch.
