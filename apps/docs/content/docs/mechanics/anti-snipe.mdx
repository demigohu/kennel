---
title: Anti self-snipe
description: The launcher's family cannot buy in the first two minutes.
---

The cheapest tokens on a bonding curve are the first ones. A launcher who buys them through a second wallet gets a big, unlocked bag at the lowest price, then sells it to everyone who arrives later.

On Kennel, for **2 minutes** after a launch, `buy` rejects the launching handler's family: the handler, its sub-accounts and its registered pups.

The only exception is the **dev buy** made inside `launch` itself. It is the launcher's honest, public buy, and it is locked by [tricks](/docs/mechanics/leash-and-tricks).
